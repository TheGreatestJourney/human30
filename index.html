Profile

The three developmental levels

1.0 · Conformist
Leans on inherited beliefs, external authority and established scripts.
2.0 · Individualist
Questions conventions, develops agency and creates a personal path.
3.0 · Synthesist
Integrates perspectives, recognizes context and creates new frameworks.

These are simplified descriptions of the framework. People may show different orientations in different quadrants and contexts.

Development cycle

Dissonance
Your current approach no longer works.
Uncertainty

Discovery
You find useful knowledge, skills and practices.
